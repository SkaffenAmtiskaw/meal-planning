# Inbox

# Purpose
The calendar header has two separate jump-to-date pickers, one for desktop and one for phones, and neither matches the design. This story builds the picker's shared day grid, with its desktop popover and phone bottom sheet, and moves both header variants onto it. The meal form ([[Meal Form Date Picker]]) builds the picker panel around the grid, and Move to / Duplicate ([[Meal Detail Modal & Edit Meals]]) reuses both later.

# Requirements
- Everything in the embedded design sections below that applies to the picker grid, its popover and bottom sheet, and the header placement.

# Out of Scope
- The meal form placement belongs to [[Meal Form Date Picker]].
- The picker's `label`, relative shortcuts, custom `shortcutItems` and `hint` moved to [[Meal Editing#Date picker options for Move / Duplicate]].
- The Move to / Duplicate placement belongs to [[Meal Detail Modal & Edit Meals]].
- Building `formatDate` and moving the calendar's existing date labels onto it belong to [[Named Date Formats]].

# Suggested Approach
*Approved by Sarah 2026-09-25 via `/assess` as part of [[Unified Date Picker Component]], and split into this story the same day. Behavior and piece numbers are the original story's, so gaps are behaviors or pieces that belong to other stories.*

## Behaviors
**Picker panel (every placement)**
1. Opens on the month of the current value, or on today's month if there's no value.
2. Previous and next arrows page by month. The grid is always 6 rows, so the panel height never changes.
3. Clicking the month title opens the month and year views.

*(Behavior 4, the Sunday week start, is built by [[Mantine Date Picker Setup]].)*

5. Day cell states: default (navy), outside month (`navy.3`, still pickable), has meals (forest dot), today (navy ring, bold), selected (sage circle). Today and selected can stack.
6. Picking a day from a neighboring month selects it and moves the view to that month.
7. Past dates can be picked.
8. Keyboard: arrows move by day or week, PgUp/PgDn by month, Home/End to the start or end of the week, Enter picks, Esc closes.

*(Behavior 9, the Today footer, is in [[Meal Form Date Picker]].)*

10. The panel only reports the pick. The placement closes it and acts on the date.
11. A day with at least one meal gets a dot. Any meal counts, and titles aren't parsed. The meal dates come from the calendar data the page already loads on the server, so there's no fetching, loading state or error state.

**Desktop and phone containers**

12. On desktop it's a popover anchored to its trigger. Mantine flips it above when there's no room below. *(Changed 2026-09-25 when the story was split: this is Mantine's default, so it gets no check of its own.)*
13. On phones it's a bottom sheet, not full screen. It has a title and a Close button, and swiping down on its handle or header, or tapping the backdrop, closes it. It clears the safe area and scrolls if it gets taller than 90% of the screen.
    - **Sheet title:** **"Jump to date"**. *(The meal form's "Choose day" is in [[Meal Form Date Picker]]. A placement's `label` as the sheet title moved to [[Meal Editing#Date picker options for Move / Duplicate]].)*
14. On phones it uses touch sizes: day cells around 50px, 44px arrows, 17px numbers, bigger dots. Pressed states replace hover.
15. On phones, swiping left or right on the grid pages months.

**Header placement**

16. On desktop the trigger is an input-styled button labeled "Date", showing the date in view as `9/24/2026`.
    - **Phones:** the period label ("September 2026 ▾") stays the trigger, as the approved design in the archived [[Mobile Month View]] note specifies.
    - **The picker:** marks the date in view as selected, shows meal dots and has no Today footer.
17. Picking a date moves the calendar to that date and closes the picker.

**Read-only users**

28. Viewers use the header picker like everyone else, and nothing behaves differently for them. They never see the meal form.

# Implementation

## Step 1: One jump-to-date for both header variants
**Idea:** Both header variants jump to a date through one `JumpToDate` component.

**Source:** Behaviors 1, 7, 10, 16 and 17; Pieces 4 (first cut), 11 and 15; Client pieces (picker grid, date trigger, jump to date); Handoff: overview (replaces the header's `DatePickerInput` / `DatePicker`); Handoff: placements image (panel 1: the Date field, its notched label and its chevron; Sarah decided 2026-10-31 to notch the label, as the image shows, over Mantine's label above the field); Handoff: One panel, three placements (row 1, and no Today footer in the header); Handoff: Anatomy (month nav, weekday row and day grid, all Mantine's); Handoff: Behavior (all placements) (past dates stay selectable); Handoff: Mantine Boundary (a thin wrapper; each consumer owns its trigger, open state and what a pick does; no `DatePickerInput`, `presets`, `minDate` or `excludeDate`); Handoff: Recommended Configuration (the header column's `value` and `onChange`). Behaviors 2 and 3, and the Phones section's "Jump by month", come from Mantine's `DatePicker` inside [[Mantine Date Picker Setup]]'s `DatesProvider`, so they get no checks.

**Approach:**
- **Picker grid, first cut (Piece 4):** `DatePickerGrid` in `src/_components/CalendarDatePicker/`, a client component wrapping Mantine `DatePicker` with the header's chevron icons. Its props take their names from Recommended Configuration (`value` and `onChange`, as ISO dates), so the picker panel [[Meal Form Date Picker]] builds can pass them straight through (Sarah's call). It opens on the value's month, or on today's when there's no value, by passing Mantine's `defaultDate`, which otherwise falls back to today (Behavior 1). It sets no `minDate` or `excludeDate`, so past dates stay pickable (Behavior 7). It only reports the pick (Behavior 10). Its day states come in Step 3.
- **Date trigger (Piece 11):** `DateTrigger` in `src/_components/DateTrigger/`, built on `InputBase component="button" pointer`, with a label and a chevron in its right section that points down while closed and up while open, as the placements image shows. It takes an ISO value and a `formatDate` style name from its caller, so [[Meal Form Date Picker]] can show its own style. A notched-label variant puts its label in a notch in the field's top border, as the placements image shows. The header turns it on, and the meal form's Date field keeps Mantine's label above the field (panel 2). The variant follows the order in `docs/style_guidelines.md`, with the one-line comment it asks for on any CSS used instead of Mantine. It has no `'use client'` of its own, since it only renders inside `JumpToDate`.
- **Jump to date (Piece 15):** `JumpToDate` in `src/app/[planner]/calendar/_components/JumpToDate/`, a client component. It holds the open state and renders the trigger it's given, with the picker grid in a Mantine `Popover` with `trapFocus`, as `DatePickerInput` sets today, so opening it moves focus into the picker and Esc closes it. It reads `selectedDate` and `navigateToDate` from the calendar context, marks the date in view as the value, and on a pick navigates there and closes (Behavior 17).
- **Header (Piece 15's refactor):** `CalendarHeader` drops both jump implementations. The desktop variant renders `JumpToDate` with a notched `DateTrigger` labeled "Date", showing the date in view in a new numeric `M/d/yyyy` style added to `formatDate` (built by [[Named Date Formats]]) (Behavior 16). The phone variant renders it with the period label button it shows today ("October 2026 ▾"), as the archived [[Mobile Month View]] design specifies.

Apart from the Date field's notched label and chevron, and the phone picker opening on the date in view rather than on today's month, both headers look and work as before.

## Step 2: Bottom sheet on phones
**Idea:** On phones, the jump-to-date picker opens in a bottom sheet instead of a popover.

**Source:** Behaviors 12 and 13 (not the swipe down, which is Step 7); Piece 10; Client pieces (adaptive popover); Handoff: Phones: Bottom Sheet (the sheet, its title and Close button, closing on the backdrop, the safe area, scrolling past 90% of the screen, and "Tapping a day is final"); Handoff: Behavior (all placements) (anchored to the trigger on desktop, a bottom sheet on mobile)

**Approach:** Piece 10: `AdaptivePopover` in `src/_components/AdaptivePopover/`, generic: it takes a trigger, the open state with a close callback, a sheet title and its content. It's a client component, since it reads `useIsMobile`. On desktop it's a Mantine `Popover` anchored to the trigger, with `trapFocus` carried over from Step 1, which flips above when there's no room below (Mantine's default, so Behavior 12 gets no check). On phones it's a bottom `Drawer` built from its compound parts (`Drawer.Root`, `Overlay`, `Content`, `Header`, `Title`, `CloseButton`, `Body`), since a plain `Drawer` can't put a handle bar above its title and Step 7 needs to reach the header. It has the handle bar at the top, then the title and a Close button, rounded top corners, and the page dimmed behind it. Its bottom padding adds `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and its content scrolls once the sheet would pass 90% of the screen's height. `JumpToDate` swaps its `Popover` for `AdaptivePopover`, titled "Jump to date".

## Step 8: Page Up, Page Down, Home and End in the picker
**Idea:** Four more keys move focus within the picker grid.

**Source:** Behavior 8 (PgUp/PgDn and Home/End; arrows, Enter and Esc are Mantine's); Piece 20; Client pieces (picker keys hook); Handoff: Behavior (all placements) (keyboard)

**Approach:** Piece 20, both with the grid in `src/_components/CalendarDatePicker/`, the function in its `_utils/` and the hook in its `_hooks/`:
- **`getKeyTargetDate(date, key)`**, pure: PgUp/PgDn give the same day in the previous or next month, clamped to that month's last day (Jan 31 → Feb 28). Home/End give the Sunday or Saturday of the date's week, through the shared `getWeekStart` that [[Mantine Date Picker Setup]] builds in `src/_utils` (Piece 2), not the dead `src/app/[planner]/calendar/_utils/getWeekStart.ts`.
- **`useDatePickerKeys`** handles those four keys on the focused day: it stops the page from scrolling, shows the target's month when it's in another month (through the controlled month from Step 6) and focuses the target day. It's client-only, since it handles keyboard events on the grid.

Arrows, Ctrl+arrows and Enter stay Mantine's, and Esc closes through the `Popover` and `Drawer` defaults.
