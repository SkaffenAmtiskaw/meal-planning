# Purpose
Help people find a recipe or bookmark in a long list, mainly by filtering on tags, and by searching for anything written in a recipe.

# Requirements
- **Default order:** the list is alphabetized by title.
- **Sort:** a menu offers A to Z, Z to A, Recently added and Recently planned. The chosen order isn't saved.
- **Tags:** tags are the main filter and sit in a side rail.
  - The rail shows the 8 most-used tags as checkboxes, with the selected ones first. A "Find a tag" field and "Show all" reach the rest.
  - Each tag shows a count of the items that have it, within the current search and Show setting.
- **Several tags:** picking several tags shows items with any of them, with those matching the most tags first. Items matching the same number of tags have title matches first while searching, then the chosen sort.
- **Matched tags:** each row fills in the tags that match the filter.
- **Search:** one search box scans the whole item.
  - For a recipe: title, ingredients, instructions, notes, tags, source and storage.
  - For a bookmark: title, notes and tags, but not its URL.
- **Search results:** a result that didn't match on its title shows which field matched and the text around the match.
- **Recipes and bookmarks:** a small "Show" dropdown under the tags offers Recipes and bookmarks, Recipes only and Bookmarks only.
- **Phone:** the tags and the Show dropdown move to a "Tags and filters" screen, opened from a button beside Sort.

# Open Decisions
1. Where do search, filters and sort go on the page?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** a side rail with tags first and the search box above the list (option E in the wireframe). Sarah's call: she expects most people to filter on tags, so tags need the most room.
     - Rejected: One toolbar (A) - gives Recipes vs Bookmarks more room than tags
     - Rejected: Filter rail (B) - gives Recipes vs Bookmarks the top of the rail; E is this layout with tags first
     - Rejected: Tabs and tag chips (C) - many tags won't fit well
     - Rejected: One smart search (D) - filters are hard to discover
     - Rejected: One line of chips with an All tags panel (F) - less common tags take an extra click, and it shows no counts until the panel opens
     - Rejected: Rail of chips (G) - harder to scan than a list, and gets long as tags grow
2. How much room does the Recipes vs Bookmarks filter get?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** a small dropdown, with less room than tags. Sarah's call: people only need to be able to find it.
3. What can people search?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** the whole recipe, in one search box, not only titles and ingredients. Sarah's call: someone with no slow cooker tag may still want the recipes that use a slow cooker.
4. How can people re-order the list, and is the order saved?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** a sort menu only, with nothing saved.
     - Rejected: Sortable columns - turns the list into a table, which is harder on a phone
     - Rejected: My order - needs saved order, drag plus up and down arrows, and a rule for moving items while a filter is on
     - Rejected: Pin to top - needs saved pins
5. When someone picks several tags, must an item have all of them or any of them?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** any of them, with items matching all of them higher in the list. Sarah's call.
     - Rejected: All of them - only the items with every selected tag would show
6. Should rows show which tags matched?
   - **Decided 2026-11-01:** yes, by filling in the matched tags on each row.
     - Rejected: Leave the row's tags unmarked - a recipe low in the list could look like it doesn't belong
