# Purpose
The recipes page is in pretty rough shape. Redesign it so it's ready for users. The redesign will spawn multiple stories. This goal also brainstorms a wish list of ideas for the page and decides which of them are worth doing and which belong in this release.

# Done When
- The recipes page is redesigned, and every recipes screen matches its approved design.
- Filtering in the recipes page is better.
- Creating a recipe has better UX.
- A research story has brainstormed a wish list of ideas for the recipes page and decided two things for each: whether it's worth doing, and whether it's in this release.
- Every piece the research story puts in this release is built.
- The recipes page is fully usable on a phone: every workflow you can do on desktop is possible on a phone.
- The recipes page is accessible: it works fully with a keyboard and a screen reader, and passes an accessibility audit against WCAG 2.2 AA.
- E2E tests cover the recipes page's major flows, including the ones this goal adds.
- A final UX and styles pass is done. It starts only once everything else here is done.
