# Drawing a Goal from a Standing Goal
The drawn goal is named `<standing goal> YYYY-MM-DD` with today's date. It goes through `SKILL.md` from step 2 to the end, like any new goal, with one difference: in step 3, before searching, offer the work that serves the standing goal. That's the lines under its heading in Later, and the lines elsewhere that carry its 🎯 link. The user's answer decides what happens to each:
- **In:** the work moves to the drawn goal. A line under the standing goal's heading moves under the drawn goal's heading. Anywhere else, the standing goal's 🎯 link on the line becomes the drawn goal's.
- **Not this time:** it stays with the standing goal, and nothing is recorded.
